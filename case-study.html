The problem

My life lived in six apps, and things kept slipping.

Texts, emails, calendar invites, Slack threads, meeting notes. Every follow-up I owed someone lived in my head, which meant I'd remember it three days late.

Most AI tools wait for you to ask. I wanted one that runs on its own: watches everything, tells me what matters and handles the work around it.

What it does

One brainEvery person, meeting, decision and promise lives in one place, so nothing starts from zero. It knows who someone is, what I owe them and when we last talked.
Watches everythingTexts, email, calendar and Slack are checked all day. It only pings me when something actually needs me: a deadline, a payment problem, a reply worth sending.
Files every callA meeting ends and it pulls out the decisions, who owns what and every promise I made, then reminds me before they're due.
Drafts in my voiceTexts, emails and Slack replies that sound like me, ready for one tap. Nothing goes out without my OK.
