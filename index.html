How It Works

Four steps, start to shipped.

01

We talk

Tell me what's frustrating, what takes too long, and what your team keeps redoing. That's the whole prep.

02

I map it

I go find where AI actually fits — and where it doesn't. You get a plain-English plan of what's worth building first.

03

I design and build the system end to end, then test it against real work from your business before it goes live.

04

You use it

I hand it over with everything your team needs to run it, and we tune it once it's been used on real work.

Who I Am

I've always been competitive. AI just gave me a new game to play.

I studied real estate and finance at Florida State and went into brokerage. I liked the business — I still do. But I kept sitting in client meetings thinking the same thing: I want to be on that side of the table.

Around the same time I got serious about AI, and got hooked. Not because it was cool, but because I could see how much work people were still doing by hand that they simply didn't need to do anymore.

Someone once asked how good I am at AI. The best comparison I had was golf: I'm around scratch. I know what I'm doing, I've put in the reps, and I'm nowhere near done getting better.

Fit

Am I the right person for this?

The best projects start with a business that has a real bottleneck and the willingness to change how something gets done.

This is a fit if…

  • Your team repeats the same manual process every week and everyone knows it.
  • You'd rather see a working system than read a strategy deck.
  • You have the problem clearly, even if you have no idea what the solution looks like.
  • You're open to changing a process, not just bolting AI onto the old one.

This probably isn't if…

  • You want AI in the business mostly so you can say there's AI in the business.
  • You've already picked the tool and just want someone to install it.
  • Nobody internally can tell me how the work actually gets done today.
  • The process has to stay exactly as it is.
