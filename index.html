Who I Am

I've always been competitive. AI just gave me a new game to play.

I studied real estate and finance at Florida State and went into brokerage. I liked the business. But I kept sitting in client meetings thinking I wanted to be on the other side of the table, building things of my own.

Then I got into AI, and got hooked. Not because it was cool, but because I could see how much work people were still doing by hand that they didn't need to do anymore.

Someone once asked how good I am at AI. Best comparison I had was golf: I'm around scratch. I've put in the reps, and I'm nowhere near done getting better.

Fit

Am I the right person for this?

This is a fit if…

  • Your team repeats the same manual process every week and everyone knows it.
  • You'd rather see a working system than read a strategy deck.
  • You know the problem, even if you have no idea what the solution looks like.
  • You're open to changing a process, not just bolting AI onto the old one.

This probably isn't if…

  • You want AI in the business mostly so you can say there's AI in the business.
  • You've already picked the tool and just want someone to install it.
  • Nobody internally can tell me how the work actually gets done today.
  • The process has to stay exactly as it is.
