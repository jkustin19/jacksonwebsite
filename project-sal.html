The Problem

My life admin lived in six apps, and things kept slipping.

Texts, emails, calendar invites, Slack threads, meeting notes. Every follow-up I owed someone lived in my head, which meant I'd remember it three days late.

Most AI assistants answer questions when you ask them. I wanted one that actually watches everything, tells me what matters, and handles the work around it.

What I Built

One brain, always on, on a short leash.

01

One brain

Every person, meeting, decision and promise lives in a structured second brain the AI reads and writes, so context never resets.

02

Always on

It runs on my laptop around the clock. I text it from my phone like a person, voice notes included.

03

Watches, then flags

It checks my messages, email, Slack threads and calendar, and only pings me when something needs me. Quiet hours cost nothing.

04

Acts with my OK

It drafts texts and emails, books calendar time, files meetings and runs reminders. Nothing goes out to anyone until I say so.

In Practice

A normal day with it.

All day

6:00 PM

“Still open today:” three things, each with a next step.
