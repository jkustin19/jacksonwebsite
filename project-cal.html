Cal: an AI teammate for a creator business

Cal runs the back office for Sammy Certifies, a fast-growing food creator brand: brand outreach, reply tracking, recipes and Slack.

The Problem

A fast-growing creator with no back office.

Sammy Certifies makes high-protein sweet potato burritos (burtatos) and food content. Brand deals, recipes, content ideas and analytics all lived in DMs and in Sammy's head.

Pitching brands one at a time took hours, nothing was tracked, and good replies sat unanswered.

What I Built

A teammate that never stops working.

01

Outreach engine

Researches brands, checks fit and conflicts, writes a personalized pitch for each, and puts it in Sammy's drafts for him to send.

02

Reply tracking

Every reply gets read and sorted (yes, later, redirect, no) and routed with the next step, so warm leads don't go cold.

03

Recipe pipeline

A recipe dropped in Slack becomes a structured record and a branded one-page card, grouped into recipe books for the website.

04

Teammate in Slack

Cal answers the team's questions from the business brain in a couple of minutes and handles content research and analytics.

In Practice

What it looks like day to day.

Outreach

Pitch, then draft, then Sammy hits send. Nothing goes out on its own.

Replies

NYC restaurants and national brands saying yes, flagged with a ready-to-send reply.

Recipes

A recipe posted in Slack in the afternoon is a finished recipe card by evening.
